What each check contributes

  1. PaddleOCR extracts visible names, bios and counts for the reporter to review.
  2. Presidio detects supported contact details; detected regions are masked before screenshots go to Meta AI.
  3. imagededup compares individual post-photo regions. Shared media alone does not establish impersonation.
  4. Meta AI describes visible similarities and differences. Text-reuse ML and spam-pattern ML provide separate signals.
  5. A moderator examines the evidence and confirms or dismisses the report.

This local hackathon prototype uses fictional profiles and stock photography. It does not modify Instagram. Text-reuse ML uses synthetic training pairs; real-account accuracy remains unvalidated. Screenshot evidence cannot prove account ownership, and automatic contact detection may miss data.
